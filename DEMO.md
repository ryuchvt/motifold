## Recording steps

1. Start the recorded walkthrough. It opens the exact accepted MusicXML. The original MIDI is a separate download. This opening does not make another bar-reading call.
2. Inspect the score and its harmony. Confirm both `Piano upper` and `Piano lower` as Piano.
3. Skip all three questions. I leave “Is the held middle note a second tune?”, “Are the added thirds part of the tune?” and “Does the piece end on bar 24?” unanswered in this prepared direction.
4. Choose Arrange and String orchestra. Keep all source notes and harmony. Leave the optional direction empty. There is no mood and no extra bar.
5. Create the two plans and their first sections. Compare A and B on the same score and hear the captured previews.
6. Finish the choice actually recorded, inspect it, keep it and export its MIDI and MusicXML. This step is a recording instruction until the real successful note replies and the chosen output are available.

The final choice and export details belong in the completed recording guide. I do not name an unwritten choice, invent a missing reply or enable the replay index early. Once the run is complete, the guide states which option is finished and whether the other has only a preview. I verify that exact path with provider requests blocked before publishing `public/demo/index.json`.

The replay is recorded and spends no credit. Other ensembles, answers, moods, chord edits or rewrites have no prepared reply unless I recorded that exact request. The replay stops on an unprepared request rather than sending it to Meta. Ordinary local score edits and exports still work.
